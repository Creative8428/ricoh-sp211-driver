The driver is installed. Print a test page to check that everything works.

If the printer is not in the printer list, connect the USB cable and add it in System Settings → Printers & Scanners → Add Printer. Choose “RICOH SP 211 (native Apple Silicon)” if it is not selected automatically.

Two-sided printing: the printer prints the even pages first. Put the stack back in the tray, printed side up, to print the odd pages.

The printer can also be used without a cable, over AirPrint, when it is connected to an OpenWrt router: see AirPrint on Router.app.
